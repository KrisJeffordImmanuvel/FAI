 4. Fuzzy-logic priority level
 AI concept: fuzzification · Mamdani rules (AND=min, OR=max) · defuzzification
══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
 Inputs: lateness = 7 days overdue · demand = 3 waiting · fine = ₹10 · risk = 0.40
 Variable │ Non-zero memberships μ 
 ─────────┼────────────────────────
 lateness │ moderately_overdue=0.40
 demand   │ high=0.50              
 fine     │ small=1.00             
 risk     │ low=0.25, medium=0.50  
 Fuzzy rule                                               │ Strength │ → Level 
 ─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┼──────────┼─────────
 F2: IF lateness is moderately_overdue AND demand is high │     0.40 │ Critical
 Aggregated: Low 0.00, Medium 0.00, High 0.00, Critical 0.40
 Level = Critical  (defuzzified crisp value 90.0)
